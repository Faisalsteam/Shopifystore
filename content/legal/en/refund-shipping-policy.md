## Returns & Exchanges

In line with Kuwait's Consumer Protection Law No. 39 of 2014:

- You may request a return or exchange within **14 days** of delivery.
- The product must be unused, in its original packaging, and in its original condition. **For health and safety reasons, opened or used products cannot be returned or exchanged**, unless there is a manufacturing defect.
- If the product is defective, we cover the full cost of return shipping.
- For returns not related to a defect, return shipping cost may be deducted from the refund unless stated otherwise at the time of purchase.

## How to Request a Return

1. Contact us at [email] or [WhatsApp] within 14 days of delivery, with your order number and reason for return.
2. We'll send you return instructions (address, and pickup arrangements if the courier handles it).
3. Once the returned product is inspected and confirmed to meet the conditions above, the refund is issued within [number of days — to be completed based on the confirmed payment gateway's policy] to the original payment method.

## Exchanges

A product may be exchanged for another (same value, or with a price difference charged/refunded) under the same conditions and timeframe above.

## Non-Returnable Items

For health reasons, opened or used products (perfumes and cosmetics) cannot be returned or exchanged, except in the case of a clear manufacturing defect.
