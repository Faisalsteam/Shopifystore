## GCC seasonal moments worth planning around

- Ramadan / Eid al-Fitr — gifting angle, earlier promotion window
- Eid al-Adha
- National Day(s) of the launch country
- Back-to-school / new season
- White Friday / Black Friday equivalent
- Valentine's Day, Mother's Day — gifting-relevant for perfumes/cosmetics

Exact dates shift yearly (lunar calendar for Islamic holidays) — confirm against the actual launch year before scheduling.

## Content types in rotation

- New arrival / restock announcement
- Best-seller spotlight
- Scent/ingredient education (ties to SEO blog content — `docs/seo/`)
- User-generated content / review highlight (once reviews exist)
- Behind-the-brand / story content
- Promo/discount push (frequency capped — avoid discount fatigue)
